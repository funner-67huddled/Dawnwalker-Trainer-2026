## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **The Blood of Dawnwalker**. This trainer provides optional quality-of-life and gameplay assistance for Coen’s human and vampire playstyles. It can protect health and stamina, support directional sword combat, preserve activation resources, accelerate skill progression, and reduce the pressure of the game’s limited story timeline.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a more relaxed single-player experience, easier experimentation with Coen’s human and vampire skill trees, faster build testing, or additional freedom when managing the game’s time-limited story.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

- **Infinite Stamina** — Keeps stamina available for blocking, dodging, movement, and sustained swordplay.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use the trainer after loading into the world. Infinite stamina supports sprinting, dodging, blocking, and extended movement without interrupting exploration. Vampire abilities that affect nighttime traversal can be tested without worrying about resource loss.

### Tools & Inventory

The trainer is intended to complement normal inventory management rather than replace it. Keep essential weapons, consumables, quest items, and equipment organized, and use skill-point or resource options only after confirming that the correct save file is active.

### World & Environment

The game takes place in the open-world region of Vale Sangora, where choices, investigations, relationships, and missed opportunities shape the story. Freeze Story Time is useful for exploring locations and preparing objectives without consuming the limited campaign schedule.

### Combat & Weapons

Daytime combat uses directional attacks, blocks, and parries with a sword, while nighttime combat emphasizes vampiric abilities and stealth. Activate God Mode and Infinite Stamina when learning enemy patterns, and use Unlimited Activation Charges to experiment with witchcraft, physical techniques, and executions.
